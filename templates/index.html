ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding Customer Churn

Customer churn occurs when an existing customer stops using a company's services. Predicting churn allows businesses to identify customers who may leave and take preventive action.

Why does churn prediction matter?

Acquiring a new customer can be significantly more expensive than retaining an existing one. Machine learning can help businesses identify customers who are at higher risk of leaving.

This project uses historical customer information to learn patterns associated with churn and uses those patterns to generate predictions for new customers.

The application provides a churn probability, allowing the result to be interpreted as an indication of potential customer retention risk.
